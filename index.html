From our kitchen

Simple herbal inspiration

Small rituals and recipes inspired by ingredients you may already know.

Fresh mint & lemon infusion

Steep washed mint leaves in hot water, add a slice of lemon and enjoy the refreshing flavour.

Warming ginger tea

Steep a few slices of fresh ginger in hot water, strain and add lemon to taste.

Recipes are shared for flavour and general lifestyle interest, not to treat or prevent medical conditions. Check suitability if you have allergies, take medication or are pregnant.
